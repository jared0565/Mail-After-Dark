# Mail After Dark privacy statement

Mail After Dark changes the appearance of websites where you enable its dark theme.

The extension does not read or collect email contents, attachments, or account credentials. It does not use analytics, advertising, remote code, or third-party services, and does not transmit user data to the publisher or other parties.

The extension accesses relevant tab and frame URLs locally to identify the website and apply its saved preference. It stores only the website patterns where you enable dark mode in the browser's local extension storage. These patterns contain the scheme and hostname, not message paths, query strings or email contents. Google classifies website domains and URLs as web browsing activity; this limited local handling is used solely to provide per-website dark mode. The extension does not keep a chronological browsing history, visit timestamps, or a record of every website you visit. These settings are not synced by this extension or sent to the publisher.

Site access is requested when you enable dark mode on a website. It is used to apply and remove the local theme. The extension does not inspect message text. Turning dark mode off deletes that website's saved preference; the browser may retain its site permission until you revoke it in the extension settings. Uninstalling removes the extension's local settings.

The browser and extension store operate under their own privacy policies.
